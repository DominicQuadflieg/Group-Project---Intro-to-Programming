NOVAFit – install it as an app on your phone

1. Drag this folder (unzipped) onto https://app.netlify.com/drop (free).
   Netlify gives you an address, e.g. https://novafit-xyz.netlify.app
2. Open the address on your iPhone in Safari (Android: Chrome).
3. iPhone: Share icon -> "Add to Home Screen".  Android: menu -> "Install app".
4. Done: the app has its own icon, opens full screen and also works offline.

First start: create your account (name, email, password) and answer four quick
questions. NOVAFit then suggests three training plans and you pick one.

Your account and data stay on the phone. Use Settings -> "Back up" now and then
to save a backup file. On a new phone choose "I already have an account" and load it.
Updates: drag the new folder into the Netlify project under "Deploys". Address and data stay the same.
Reminders: Settings -> choose days + time -> "Add to calendar".

Updating from an older version: all training data is kept. Existing users are asked to
create an account once; the questionnaire can be skipped.
iPhone: the name and icon on the home screen only change after removing the app and adding
it again. Make a backup first (Settings -> "Back up") and load it afterwards, because removing
the app from the home screen deletes its data on the phone.
